# Product plan: Freelancer money manager

## What we're building

A web app that gives a solo freelancer one place to answer four questions:

1. **What have I earned and spent?** (income and expenses)
2. **Who owes me money?** (invoices)
3. **How much should I set aside for tax?** (tax estimates)
4. **Am I okay?** (cash flow and profit)

**Target user:** a solo freelancer or contractor (designer, developer, writer, consultant) with 1–15 clients and irregular income. They currently use spreadsheets plus a separate invoicing tool.

**Positioning:** simpler than full accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero) and more complete than an invoicing-only tool. It's built around the freelancer's irregular income, not a small business's books.

**Core promise:** "Send an invoice, get paid, and always know how much of it is actually yours after tax."

## Scope decisions

These shape everything else, so I'd settle them first:

- **Single currency, single jurisdiction at launch.** Tax rules differ a lot by country. I'd pick one (e.g. US self-employed) and build the tax engine for it. The code would keep tax rules in a swappable module.
- **No bank-sync in v1.** Manual entry plus CSV import gets us to a usable product much faster. Bank feeds (Plaid or similar) come in v2.
- **Cash-basis tracking.** Income counts when paid, not when invoiced. This is how most freelancers file, and it's simpler.
- **Tax figures are estimates, not advice.** The UI says so clearly.

## Features

### MVP (v1)

**Clients**
- Client list with contact details, default payment terms, and a currency
- Per-client totals: billed, paid, outstanding

**Invoicing**
- Create invoices with line items, tax/discount, due date, and notes
- Auto-numbering and a PDF export
- Send by email with a shareable link to a public invoice page
- Statuses: draft, sent, viewed, overdue, paid, void
- Record full or partial payments
- Duplicate an invoice and set up recurring invoices
- Automatic payment reminders

**Income and expenses**
- Log income, either linked to an invoice or standalone
- Log expenses with category, date, vendor, notes, and a receipt upload
- Tax-deductible flag and business-use percentage (e.g. 60% of a phone bill)
- Recurring expenses (subscriptions, rent)
- CSV import for bank and card exports
- Search, filter, and bulk edit

**Tax set-aside**
- Year-to-date net profit, computed from income minus deductible expenses
- Estimated quarterly tax payments with due dates
- A "set aside X% of every payment" indicator, so each payment shows what's safe to spend
- Configurable settings: filing status, tax rate overrides, and the region's rules

**Dashboard and reports**
- Cash in vs. out, profit this month and year to date
- Outstanding and overdue invoices
- Tax set-aside balance
- Reports: profit and loss, income by client, expenses by category, with CSV export

**Account**
- Email/password and Google sign-in
- Business profile (name, logo, address, tax ID) used on invoices

### v2 (later)

- Bank sync and auto-categorization
- Online payments on invoices (Stripe) with automatic reconciliation
- Time tracking and project-based billing
- Mileage tracking
- Multi-currency
- Accountant access (read-only share) and a year-end tax package export
- Proposals and contracts
- Income forecasting, since freelance income is lumpy
- Additional tax jurisdictions

### Explicitly out of scope

Payroll, inventory, double-entry bookkeeping, filing taxes on the user's behalf, and multi-user teams.

|---|---|---|

**Navigation:** a left sidebar with Dashboard, Invoices, Clients, Transactions, Taxes, Reports, and Settings. A global "+ New" button creates an invoice, expense, or income entry from anywhere.

## Core data model

- **User / Business**: profile, tax settings, defaults
- **Client**: contact details, terms
- **Invoice** → **LineItems**, **Payments**

- **Scope creep toward full accounting:** the out-of-scope list above is there to hold that line.
